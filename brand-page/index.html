A collaboration I’d love to make

What I wore this week as a university professor.

A repeatable, useful style series for men who want to dress for the life they actually have: class on Monday, coffee with a founder on Tuesday, a family outing on Saturday.

Each short video or carousel shows real outfits in real places, with simple styling notes: what fits, what works together, and how to look current without feeling like you’re dressing as somebody else.

I would feature your pieces naturally across the week, tag the brand, and make the content useful enough to save and share. We can shape the deliverables and usage rights around your campaign.
